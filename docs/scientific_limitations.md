# Scientific Limitations & Research Methodology Disclosure

**Project Title:** Human Behavior Aware Micro-Zone Disaster Prediction System Using AI  
**Academic Context:** Final-Year B.Tech / M.Tech AIML Capstone Project  
**Author / Engineering Team:** AIML Development Team  

---

## 1. Scope & Primary Disclaimer

This application is an **academic decision-support research prototype** designed to explore predictive modeling of localized hydrometeorological hazard events using Deep Learning (Stacked LSTM networks). 

> **CRITICAL NOTICE:** This system is NOT an official governmental early-warning service, certified disaster management agency, or life-safety broadcast system. Operational outputs are probabilistic decision-support estimates and should not be used as the sole basis for emergency evacuation orders without verification by authorized meteorological bodies.

---

## 2. Human Behavior Data Limitation (Mandatory Disclosure)

### 2.1 The Context of the Title
The research project is titled *"Human Behavior Aware Micro-Zone Disaster Prediction System Using AI"*. A central tenet of scientific honesty in machine learning research is to transparently report the provenance, nature, and limits of training features rather than fabricating or overstating analytical capabilities.

### 2.2 Absence of Direct Behavioral Feeds in Initial Pipeline
The initial operational pipeline focuses strictly on verified **historical hydrometeorological time series** (precipitation, barometric pressure, wind speed, relative humidity, surface temperature, and soil moisture saturation). It does **NOT** ingest:
- Real-time CCTV surveillance video or crowd computer vision feeds.
- Cellular GPS tracking or mobile phone localization logs.
- Social media platform text scraping or sentiment indicators.
- Live biometric or wearable sensor data.

### 2.3 Rationale and Future Work Roadmap
1. **Privacy & Ethical Safeguards:** Real-time human crowd monitoring in disaster zones introduces significant ethical considerations, data governance hurdles, and personal identifiable information (PII) compliance requirements.
2. **Data Availability:** Authentic, high-resolution behavioral tracking datasets coincident with localized flash flood events are scarce and rarely available in open-access academic environments.
3. **Structured Roadmap:** Pedestrian evacuation density modeling, shelter occupancy feedback, and agent-based crowd movement simulations are formally designated as **Prospective Phase II Modules** to be introduced when privacy-compliant, sensor-integrated testbeds become accessible.

## 3. Data Integrity & Label Definitions

### 3.1 Ground Truth Hazard Definition
Disaster hazard events in the historical dataset are defined by objective physical thresholds grounded in hydrological literature:
- Cumulative single-hour rainfall exceeding **45.0 mm** (cloudburst / flash-flood threshold).
- Rainfall exceeding **25.0 mm** combined with soil saturation exceeding **75.0%** (antecedent moisture saturation flood hazard).
- Surface wind speeds exceeding **75.0 km/h** accompanied by barometric pressure falling below **995.0 hPa** (cyclonic depression hazard).

### 3.2 Distinction of Data Types
The system strictly distinguishes between three data tiers:
1. **Historical Observations:** Ground-truth meteorological measurements logged by environmental observation stations.
2. **Model Predictions:** Prospective 24-hour horizon hazard probability scores generated by the trained LSTM network based on the preceding 24 hours of sequence history.
3. **Illustrative Demonstrations:** Zone boundary geometries represented as geographic circular radii rather than official cadastral boundary polygons.

---

## 4. Leakage Prevention in Time-Series Machine Learning

To guarantee scientific reproducibility:
1. **Chronological Splitting:** The dataset is partitioned chronologically (70% Training, 15% Validation, 15% Testing). Shuffled k-fold cross-validation is intentionally avoided to prevent temporal lookahead leakage.
2. **Scaler Isolation:** Feature scalers (`MinMaxScaler`) are fitted **exclusively on the training partition**. The test partition remains strictly untouched during preprocessing.
3. **Baseline Comparison:** The Stacked LSTM model is systematically benchmarked against an interpretable baseline model (Logistic Regression on flattened sequence features) to quantify genuine non-linear time-series representation benefits.
